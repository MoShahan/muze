What is Muze

Your library stays on disk. Muze only listens.

Muze is a personal music player for Android built with Expo. It reads audio already on the device, groups it into Songs, Albums, and Artists, and remembers only small user data - playlists, favorites, and recents.

It does not copy, download, or store music files. There is no backend, no accounts, and no cloud sync. The target library is about a thousand tracks, mostly FLAC, with scanning and browsing kept responsive.

  • Offline first
  • FLAC friendly
  • Neomorphic UI
  • Waveform seek
  • Home widget

Features

Everything you need for local listening.

Library

  • Scan device audio (MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG, and more)
  • Sort by title, artist, date added, or duration
  • Per-screen search - no separate global search page
  • Rescan and keep local metadata current
  • Folder visibility for what counts in the library

Playback

  • Vinyl-style now-playing with rotation while playing
  • Waveform seek instead of a flat progress bar
  • Resume last track at the exact saved position
  • Shuffle, repeat, reorderable queue, sleep timer
  • Background play, lock screen, headset and Bluetooth keys
  • Android home-screen widget with art and transport

Collections

  • Create, rename, and delete playlists
  • Built-in Favorites
  • Recently played history

Personalization

  • System, Light, and Dark themes
  • Monochrome dark-gray and muted-white palette
  • Soft neomorphic surfaces and circular controls

Honest scope

What Muze is not.

Out of scope for v1

  • Streaming, accounts, social, or cloud sync
  • Lyrics, equalizer, radio, video, podcasts
  • Copying audio files into the app

Privacy by design

Music never leaves your device through Muze. There is no sign-in and no analytics backend in the product story - just local playback and small on-device user data.
